Detailed Service Breakdown

Building Construction

Our building construction service covers every phase of the construction lifecycle. We use industry-best practices, quality materials, and experienced professionals to deliver structures that stand the test of time.

  • Residential building construction (duplexes, bungalows, story buildings)
  • Commercial & industrial structures
  • Foundation engineering & structural integrity
  • Roofing, plastering & finishing works
  • Project management & supervision

Interior Design

We create beautiful, functional interior spaces tailored to your lifestyle. Whether it's a cozy living room, a productive office, or a luxurious bedroom, we bring your vision to life.

  • Space planning & layout optimization
  • Furniture selection & custom design
  • Wall treatments, paint & wallpaper
  • Lighting design & fixture selection
  • Kitchen & bathroom remodeling

Aluminium Technology

We specialize in designing and installing premium aluminium fixtures that enhance both the functionality and aesthetics of your building. Our installations are durable, weather-resistant, and beautifully finished.

  • Sliding & casement aluminium windows
  • Aluminium doors & entrance systems
  • Curtain wall systems & facades
  • Shop front & partitioning
  • Aluminium roofing & cladding

P.O.P Ceiling

Our POP ceiling designs transform ordinary rooms into extraordinary spaces. We create custom ceiling designs that complement your interior while adding value to your property.

  • Flat & tray ceiling designs
  • Multi-level ceiling with cove lighting
  • Decorative cornices & ceiling roses
  • LED-integrated ceiling systems
  • Soundproof & fireproof ceiling options

Wrought Iron & Stainless Handrail

We fabricate and install beautiful, durable wrought iron works and stainless steel handrails that provide security without compromising on style.

  • Custom wrought iron gates & fences
  • Decorative window protectors
  • Stainless steel staircase handrails
  • Balcony balustrades & railings
  • Ornamental iron art & furniture

Surveying & Estate Management

Our surveying and estate management division provides comprehensive real estate services for individuals, developers, and organizations throughout Nigeria.

  • Topographic & cadastral surveys
  • Land boundary demarcation
  • Property valuation & appraisal
  • Estate development & management
  • Building plan approval assistance
